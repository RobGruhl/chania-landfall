## Getting in from Souda
- [high] Souda is the deep-water port about 7 km east of Chania's old town. Cruise ships use the passenger/ferry quays at Souda (OSM ferry terminal 35.48979, 24.07291). https://www.greeka.com/crete/chania/ports/
- [medium] On cruise days the port shuttle runs from the Souda terminal gate to central Chania. The usual drop-off is by the Municipal Market (Agora) or Kydonias bus station / 1866 Square, about 10 minutes' walk from the Venetian harbour. Ask the ship where Virgin's shuttle stops. https://www.dorisvisits.com/souda-bay/
- [medium] A port shuttle seat is reported at about €5–6 return per person. The ride takes 15–25 minutes. https://cruisealert.com/content/port-guide/souda-chania-crete
- [medium] City bus line 13 (blue Chania city bus) runs Souda port to Chania about every 20 minutes from 06:00. It takes about 24 minutes and ends at the Agora (Sofokli Venizelou), with a stop near 1866 Square. https://www.rome2rio.com/s/So%C3%BAda/Chania
- [medium] The fare is about €1.60 from a kiosk or machine, or about €2.50 if you buy on board. The Souda port bus stop is at 35.48905, 24.07422 (OSM "ΣΟΥΔΑ ΛΙΜΑΝΙ"). https://www.rome2rio.com/s/So%C3%BAda/Chania
- [low] A taxi from Souda to the old town costs about €15–25 and takes 15–20 minutes. Agree the fare or check the meter. https://cruise-port.com/en/ports/souda-cruise-port

## Lighthouse breakwater
- [medium] The breakwater is about 600 m from its start to the lighthouse, 8–10 minutes on foot from the Agios Nikolaos bastion. Allow 15–20 minutes from the middle of the harbour. https://www.oreotravel.com/blog/chania-lighthouse/
- [medium] You can walk right up to the lighthouse but not go inside. Past the bastion the path is uneven stone and slippery in wind or wet, so skip it in today's thunderstorms or spray. https://www.kupi.com/en/explore/greece/chania/chania-lighthouse
